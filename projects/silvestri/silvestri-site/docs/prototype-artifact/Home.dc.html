Straight answers

What clients ask first

Can you represent me in a Florida court?

Not as Florida counsel. Lorenzo Silvestri is an Italian attorney and a Foreign Legal Consultant certified in Italian law by The Florida Bar, not admitted to practise law in Florida. We act as Italian-law counsel and run the Florida side with affiliated licensed firms — one file, one strategy.

Can you form and then run my Florida company?

Yes. Registration, domiciliation, registered agent, corporate books, by-laws and shareholders' agreements — and, during the start-up phase, appointment as local officer or manager and assistance with the bank account under your written instructions.

I inherited property in Italy. Can you handle it from Miami?

That is core work: Italian civil, family and estate matters, with the Rome office and its tax and accounting department, managed on your time zone and in your language.

Which languages do you work in?

Italian, English, Spanish and French. Documents, negotiations and hearings — no interpreter between you and your lawyer.
